Discipline & Harmony

Hostel Rules & Policies

Clear policies to ensure peace, safety, and mutual respect for all residents.

Security Deposit & Refund ▼
A refundable security deposit of 2 months' rent is collected at check-in. The deposit is refunded via bank transfer upon vacating, after clearance of room inspection and handover of keys.
Notice Period for Vacating ▼
Residents must provide a minimum of 30 days prior notice via WhatsApp/written format before vacating to ensure smooth account settlement.
Gate Timings & Curfew ▼
Main gates close at 10:00 PM for safety. Residents having late college projects, lab hours, or company night shifts must inform the warden in advance.
Visitor & Parent Policy ▼
Parents and guardians are warmly welcomed in the ground-floor visiting lounge between 8:00 AM and 7:30 PM. For privacy and security, outside visitors (especially of the opposite gender) are strictly not permitted inside resident bedrooms.
Quiet Hours & Strict Zero Substance Policy ▼
Post 10:30 PM is designated quiet study hours. Smoking, alcohol, tobacco, and illegal substances are strictly prohibited inside the hostel premises.
